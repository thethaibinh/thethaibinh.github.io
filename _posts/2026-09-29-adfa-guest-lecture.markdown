Invited to deliver a **guest lecture on drone and quadrotor control** to military cadets at the **Australian Defence Force Academy (ADFA), UNSW Canberra**, building on [published robotics research](/publications/) and work developing [decentralised drone swarm frameworks for defence]({% post_url 2025-07-01-drone-swarming-digital-twin %}).

The lecture, ***From Root Locus to Rotors: How a Canberra autopilot flies a million aircraft***, was part of **ZEIT3505 Flight Dynamics and Control**, linking the classical control theory cadets learn in class to the open-source autopilots flying million drones today.
